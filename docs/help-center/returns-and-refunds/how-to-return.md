# How to return an item

1. Open **My Orders**, choose the item, and tap **Return**. Or tell us in chat with your order ID.
2. Pick a reason. We check that the item is within its return window (see *Return and replacement policy*).
3. We schedule a **free pickup** for the next day between 10:00 and 14:00 and create the return label. You don't
   need to print anything.
4. Pack the item with its tags, accessories and original box if you have it, and hand it to the courier.
5. Your refund starts as soon as the item is picked up and checked. See *How long do refunds take?*.

Items that are used, damaged by the customer, or missing parts may not be accepted.
