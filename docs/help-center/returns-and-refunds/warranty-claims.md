# Warranty claims

Electronics and appliances come with the manufacturer's warranty, usually 1 year from the invoice date. The details
are on the product page and the warranty card in the box.

- **Within 30 days of delivery:** report the problem to us. We treat it as a defective item and replace or refund it.
- **After 30 days:** contact the brand's authorised service centre with your invoice. Download it from
  **My Orders → Invoice**.

If a service centre refuses a valid claim, send us the job sheet and we'll follow up with the brand for you.
