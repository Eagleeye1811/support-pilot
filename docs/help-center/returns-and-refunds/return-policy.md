# Return and replacement policy

**Change of mind:** you can return most items within the window for their category, counted from the delivery date.

| Category | Return window | Nova Premium |
|---|---|---|
| Electronics | 10 days | 30 days |
| Fashion | 30 days | 30 days |
| Home & Kitchen | 15 days | 30 days |
| Books | 10 days | 30 days |
| Beauty & Personal care | 7 days | 7 days |
| Grocery | 3 days | 3 days |

Beauty and Grocery keep their standard windows for everyone, for hygiene and freshness reasons.

**Our mistake:** wrong, damaged or defective items reported within **30 days of delivery** qualify for a free
replacement or a full refund, in any category.

Return pickups are always free. We schedule a pickup for the next day, between 10:00 and 14:00, and generate the
return label for you.
