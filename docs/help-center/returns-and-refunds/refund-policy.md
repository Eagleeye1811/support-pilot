# Refund policy

- Refunds always go back to your **original payment method**. We can't move a refund to a different account or card.
- **Duplicate charges** are always refunded in full.
- Refunds **up to ₹10,000** can be issued right away by our support assistant. Larger refunds are reviewed by our
  Payments team before they are released, usually within one working day. You're notified when it's approved.
- For returns, the refund starts once the item is picked up. For cancellations and failed payments, it starts
  immediately.

How long the money takes to reach you depends on the payment method. See *How long do refunds take?*.
