# How long do refunds take? Timelines and tracking

Once a refund is initiated, you'll see it in **My Orders → Refunds** with a refund ID (starting with **RF**) and an
expected date.

| Payment method | Expected time |
|---|---|
| UPI | within 3 days |
| NovaPay wallet | within 3 days |
| Credit / debit card | within 7 days |
| Net banking | within 7 days |

If the expected date has passed and you still don't see the money, message us with your order or refund ID. Our
Payments team raises a trace with your bank and shares the bank reference number (ARN). You can quote the ARN to
your bank to locate the credit.
