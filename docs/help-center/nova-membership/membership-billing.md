# Membership billing date and payment method

Your membership renews every 30 days from the day it was first activated. We remind you 3 days before each renewal.

To change the card or UPI ID used for renewals, go to **Account → Membership → Payment method**. If a renewal
payment fails, we retry once the next day. If the retry also fails, your membership pauses until you update the
payment method.
