# Cancelling or renewing your membership

- **Cancel any time** under **Account → Membership → Turn off auto-renew**, or ask us. Your benefits continue
  until the end of the period you've already paid for. Nothing more is charged after that.
- We don't refund the part of a month that's left, because the benefits stay active until the renewal date.
- **Reactivate** a lapsed or cancelled membership from the same screen, or ask us. It restarts on your saved payment
  method and renews every 30 days.
