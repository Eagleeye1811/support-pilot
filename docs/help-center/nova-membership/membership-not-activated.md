# I paid but my membership isn't active

After payment, your membership normally activates within a few minutes. If it hasn't:

1. Close and reopen the app, or log out and back in. Benefits are loaded when you sign in.
2. Still showing the free plan? Message us. We check your payment and push the activation again.
3. If the activation still fails, our Engineering team activates it manually and confirms with you. You won't be
   charged twice.

Your benefit period starts from the moment it's activated, not from the payment date.
