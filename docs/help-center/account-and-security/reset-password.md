# Resetting your password

Choose **Forgot password** on the login screen, or ask us in chat. We send a secure reset link to your
**registered email address**.

- The link is valid for **30 minutes** and works once.
- If you think someone else knows your password, ask us to **sign out all devices** at the same time.
- Choose a password you haven't used on other websites.

We never ask you to tell us your password.
