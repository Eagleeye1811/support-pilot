# If you think your personal data was exposed

If you think your ShopNova account details or personal information have been exposed, tell us. Our Security Incident
Response team investigates every report.

What you can do now:
- Change your ShopNova password, and any other account where you used the same one.
- Be alert to messages that pretend to be from us (see *Staying safe from fake ShopNova calls and messages*).

If we confirm an incident that affects you, we inform you and the Data Protection Board within the timelines the law
sets.
