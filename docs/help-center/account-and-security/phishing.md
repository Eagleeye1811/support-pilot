# Staying safe from fake ShopNova calls and messages

ShopNova will **never**:
- ask for your OTP, card PIN, CVV or UPI PIN
- ask you to install screen-sharing apps
- offer refunds that need you to "receive" a payment request

Our messages come only from the ShopNova app, from emails ending in **@shopnova.in**, and from SMS headers starting
with *SHPNVA*. If you've shared details with someone, report it to us right away, and we'll secure your account.
