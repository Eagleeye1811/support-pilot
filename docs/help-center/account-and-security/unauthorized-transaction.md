# I see a transaction I didn't make

If you don't recognise a payment on your ShopNova account, tell us immediately:

1. We place a **security hold** on your account at once. Saved cards are blocked and all devices are signed out, so
   nothing else can be charged.
2. Our **Trust & Safety** team reviews the transaction and contacts you on your registered phone number.
3. If the transaction is confirmed as fraudulent, we work with the payment provider to reverse it.

Please also report it on the national cybercrime helpline **1930** or at cybercrime.gov.in. **Never share OTPs, PINs
or full card numbers with anyone**, including people who say they are from ShopNova.
