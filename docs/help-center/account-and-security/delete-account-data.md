# Deleting your account and personal data

You can ask us to delete your account and personal data under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

- Send the request in chat or to privacy@shopnova.in from your registered email.
- Our **Legal & Privacy** team acknowledges it within 24 hours and confirms your identity.
- We delete your data within 30 days. We keep only what the law requires us to retain, such as tax invoices.
- Legal notices and consumer-forum matters are handled only by Legal & Privacy. Our support assistant passes them on
  without responding to their substance.
