# Updating your phone number or email

Go to **Account → Profile → Edit**. We send an OTP to the *new* number or email to confirm it, and a notice to the
old one.

If you no longer have access to your old number, message us. We verify your identity with a recent order before
changing it. For your security, contact details are never changed during the same chat in which a password reset
was requested.
