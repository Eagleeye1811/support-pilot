# I can't log in

**Account locked?** After **5 failed password attempts** your account is locked for your protection. Message us. Once
we've verified it's you, we unlock it and send a password reset link.

**OTP not arriving?**
- Wait 60 seconds before asking for a new OTP. Each new request cancels the previous code.
- Check that your phone has signal and that SMS from *SHPNVA* isn't blocked.
- Try **Get OTP on email** instead.

**On a security hold?** If we placed a protective hold after reporting suspicious activity, only our Trust & Safety
team can lift it. They contact you on your registered phone number.
