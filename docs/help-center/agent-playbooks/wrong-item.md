# Playbook: wrong item delivered

Wrong items reported within 30 days get a free replacement and pickup; claims above ₹25,000 need Fulfilment approval first. The dispatch scan is the evidence. If it matches the order, do not auto-resolve: escalate to Fulfilment with the customer's photo for verification.
