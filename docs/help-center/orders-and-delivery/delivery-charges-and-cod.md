# Delivery charges and Cash on Delivery

**Delivery charges**
- Orders of ₹499 and above: free delivery
- Orders below ₹499: ₹40 delivery charge
- Nova Plus and Nova Premium members: free delivery on every order

**Cash on Delivery (COD)**
- Available for orders up to ₹10,000
- A ₹20 handling fee applies to COD orders
- Please keep the exact amount ready. Delivery partners may not carry change.
- COD is not available for gift cards or Nova memberships.
