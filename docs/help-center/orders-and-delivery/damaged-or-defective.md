# My item arrived damaged or isn't working

If your product arrived broken, damaged in transit, or stopped working, report it **within 30 days of delivery**. We'll
replace it, or refund it if no replacement is available.

- Please share a photo of the damage and of the outer packaging if you still have it.
- **Items up to ₹25,000:** we arrange the replacement and a free pickup straight away.
- **Items above ₹25,000** (laptops, phones, large appliances): we first collect the item for a quick quality check.
  Our Fulfilment team approves the replacement or refund after the check.

After 30 days, the manufacturer's warranty applies. See *Warranty claims*.
