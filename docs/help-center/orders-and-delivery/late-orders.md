# My order is late

If your order is **one day or more past its promised delivery date**, we take two steps straight away:

1. **We expedite it.** We raise a priority request with the courier and give you a new expected date, usually the next day.
2. **We apologise with a coupon.** You receive a ₹100 coupon (₹200 for Nova Premium members), valid for 60 days on
   your next order.

We only do this once per order. If a courier has a wider disruption (for example, during floods), we may also move
pending shipments to another courier.

If the courier marked the parcel as delivered but you don't have it, see *Order shows delivered but I didn't get it*.
