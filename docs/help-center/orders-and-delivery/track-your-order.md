# Where is my order? Tracking and delivery times

Every order gets a tracking number (AWB) as soon as it is handed to the courier. You'll find it in **My Orders → Track**
and in the shipping email and SMS.

**Usual delivery times**
- Metro cities: 2–4 days after dispatch
- Other cities and towns: 4–7 days after dispatch
- Orders are packed and dispatched within 24 hours of being placed (excluding Sundays and public holidays)

**What the statuses mean**
| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Placed | We have your order and payment |
| Packed | Your items are packed and waiting for the courier |
| Shipped | The courier has your parcel; tracking is live |
| Out for delivery | The parcel is with the delivery partner in your area today |
| Delivered | The courier recorded the delivery |

If tracking hasn't moved for 48 hours, or your order is past its promised date, message us. See *My order is late*.
