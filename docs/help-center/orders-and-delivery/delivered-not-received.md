# Order shows delivered but I didn't get it

Sometimes a courier marks a parcel as delivered a little early, or hands it to a neighbour or a security desk.

Before you contact us:
- Check with family members, neighbours and your building's reception or security.
- Look for a delivery photo or an OTP note in the tracking page.
- Wait until the end of the day. Some couriers update the status before the actual handover.

If it still hasn't turned up, message us within 3 days of the delivery date. We open a trace with the courier, and a
member of our Fulfilment team contacts you. Depending on what the courier finds, you get either the parcel or a
replacement or refund.
