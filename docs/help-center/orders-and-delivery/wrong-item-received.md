# I received the wrong item

We're sorry. If you received a different product, size or colour from what you ordered, tell us **within 30 days of
delivery**. You don't pay anything to fix it.

1. Share your order ID. A photo of what you received helps us confirm it quickly.
2. We check the warehouse dispatch scan against your order.
3. When the mix-up is confirmed, we send a **free replacement**. It ships the next day for Nova Premium members and
   within 3 days for everyone else. If the item is out of stock, we refund you in full instead.
4. We schedule a **free pickup** of the wrong item for the next day, between 10:00 and 14:00. Just hand it over in
   its packaging.

For items above ₹25,000, our Fulfilment team verifies the claim before the replacement ships.
