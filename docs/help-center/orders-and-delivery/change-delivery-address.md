# Changing the delivery address

You can change the delivery address while your order is still **Placed** or **Packed**: open **My Orders → Order
details → Change address**.

Once the order has **shipped**, the address is fixed with the courier and we can't change it. You can:
- ask the courier to hold the parcel at their nearest hub (use the link in your tracking SMS), or
- refuse the delivery. The order comes back to us and is refunded automatically once it arrives.
