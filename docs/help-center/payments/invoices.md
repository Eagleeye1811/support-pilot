# Downloading an invoice

Invoices are available once your order has shipped. Open **My Orders → Order details → Download invoice**.

To get a GST invoice with your business details, add your GSTIN under **Account → Business details** *before*
placing the order. We can't add a GSTIN to an invoice after the order has shipped.
