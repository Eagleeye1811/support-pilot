# EMI options

You can pay in instalments on orders of **₹3,000 and above** using a credit card from a supported bank. Choose
**EMI** at checkout to see the available plans (3, 6, 9 or 12 months) and the interest your bank charges.

- No-cost EMI is offered on selected products. The bank's interest is given back as an upfront discount.
- If you cancel or return an EMI order, the refund goes to your card. Your bank then closes or adjusts the EMI plan.
  Some banks charge a pre-closure fee.
