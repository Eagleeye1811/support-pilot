# Payment methods we accept

- UPI (any UPI app)
- Credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, RuPay)
- Net banking from all major banks
- NovaPay wallet
- Cash on Delivery, for orders up to ₹10,000 (see *Delivery charges and Cash on Delivery*)
- EMI on credit cards for orders of ₹3,000 and above (see *EMI options*)

For your security, we never ask for your card PIN, CVV or OTP over chat, phone or email.
