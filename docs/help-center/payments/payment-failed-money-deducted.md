# Payment failed but money was deducted

Don't worry: if a payment fails after your bank has debited you, the money comes back. You don't need to pay again
for the same order unless you want to place it right away.

- Most of these debits are **reversed automatically** by the bank within a few days.
- If you share your **order ID**, we check the payment gateway and your bank's debit. When the debit is confirmed,
  we start the refund ourselves, so you don't have to wait for the automatic reversal.
- Refund timing depends on the payment method. See *How long do refunds take?*.

If the payment actually succeeded and only the order screen says *payment pending*, we reconcile it and confirm your
order instead.
