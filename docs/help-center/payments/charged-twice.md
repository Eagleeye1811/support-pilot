# I was charged twice

If you see two debits for one order, share your order ID and we'll check both transactions with the payment gateway.

- **Two successful captures:** we refund the duplicate in full straight away.
- **One capture and one pending or authorisation entry:** the second entry is a temporary hold by your bank. It
  disappears without any action within 7 days, and no money is taken.

You'll get the refund ID for any duplicate we refund.
