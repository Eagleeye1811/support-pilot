# Raising a complaint or grievance

If you're unhappy with how an issue was handled, reply on the ticket or ask for a **supervisor**. Your ticket moves
to our Escalation Desk.

If it's still unresolved after 7 days, you can write to our **Grievance Officer** at grievance@shopnova.in with your
ticket number. We respond within 48 hours and resolve the grievance within 30 days, as required by the Consumer
Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020.
