# Rating your support experience

After an issue is resolved we ask you to rate it from 1 to 5 stars. Ratings are read by our quality team every week.
Low ratings are reviewed individually, and recurring problems are taken up with the team that owns them.

Ratings also tune our support assistant: issue types that customers rate poorly after automatic handling are routed
to human specialists more often.
