# How quickly will I hear back?

When your issue goes to a specialist, you get a ticket number (starting with **TKT-**). A first response is promised
within these times:

| Issue priority | Standard | Nova Plus | Nova Premium |
|---|---|---|---|
| Critical (fraud, security) | 1 hour | 30 minutes | 15 minutes |
| High | 2 hours | 1 hour | 15 minutes |
| Medium | 4 hours | 2 hours | 30 minutes |
| Low | 8 hours | 4 hours | 1 hour |

You're updated on your chosen channel every time the ticket changes status. Ask *what's the status of my ticket* any
time.
