# Contacting support and support hours

Our support assistant is available **24×7** on website chat, WhatsApp, Telegram, email and the ShopNova app. It can
check your orders, payments and account and fix most issues straight away.

Human specialists work **8:00–22:00, every day**. If an issue needs one, the assistant hands it over with the full
details, so you won't need to repeat yourself. You can ask for a person at any time by saying *talk to a human*.
