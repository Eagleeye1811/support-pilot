# My NovaFiber internet isn't working

1. **Check for an outage.** Message us. We check the outage map for your area. If there is one, we link your line
   to it and tell you the expected restoration time. We also notify you when service is back.
2. **Restart the router.** Switch it off at the wall for 30 seconds, then on again. Wait 3 minutes for the lights to
   settle.
3. **Check the lights.** A red *LOS* light means the fibre signal isn't reaching your router. That needs us, not a
   restart.
4. **We can reset your line remotely.** If there's no outage, we run a remote diagnosis and reset.
5. **Technician visit.** If the remote reset doesn't fix it (for example, a weak optical signal), we book a free
   technician visit. See *Technician visits*.

Keep your service ID handy. It starts with **SRV** and is printed on your bill.
