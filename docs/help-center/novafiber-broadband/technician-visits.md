# Technician visits

When an issue can't be fixed remotely, we book a **free technician visit**. The usual slot is the next day between
10:00 and 12:00.

- You receive the technician's name and a **4-digit OTP**. Share the OTP only with the technician when they arrive.
  This confirms the visit is genuine.
- Please make sure someone aged 18 or over is at home and the router is accessible.
- Visits for faults on our network are free. Damage to the router caused by power surges or liquid may be charged,
  and we always tell you before any work starts.
